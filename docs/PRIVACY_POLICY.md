# LCB Pad Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** July 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information LCB Pad ("the Bot") collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding that information.

## 1. Information Collected

LCB Pad stores only the information necessary to provide its functionality.

The Bot stores:

* Your Discord User ID, which is used to associate saved data with your account.
* Skills and related data that you choose to save using the Bot's commands.

The Bot may receive information provided through Discord when commands are used, but it does not intentionally store personal information such as your real name, email address, IP address, or message content outside of what is required to process commands.

## 2. How Your Information Is Used

The stored information is used solely to:

* Save and retrieve your skills.
* Provide the Bot's commands and features.
* Maintain the functionality of the Bot.

Your information is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties.

## 3. Data Storage

Saved skill data is stored using Supabase to provide the Bot's features.

Reasonable efforts are made to protect stored information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

## 4. Data Retention

Your saved data is retained until:

* You delete it using the Bot's commands, where applicable.
* It is removed by the developer.
* The Bot is discontinued.

## 5. Your Rights

You may request the deletion of your stored data by contacting the developer through the official LCB Pad support server. An invite link to the support server is available in the Bot's description.

## 6. Third-Party Services

LCB Pad operates through Discord and uses Supabase for data storage. Your use of the Bot is also subject to the privacy policies and terms of those services.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the Bot after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

## 8. Contact

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to request deletion of your stored data, please contact the developer through the official LCB Pad support server. The invite link is available in the Bot's description.
